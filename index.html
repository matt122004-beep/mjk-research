The Models Speak... But Do We Listen?

Leave models by themselves without a task and they say quite a lot. 

01

Recording the behavior

With over 32,000 conversations conducted and recorded, I regularly expose over 100+ models from a wide number of labs to various testing setups which elicit unprompted conversations. 

02

Pushing inside the model

Once the activity is recognized at scale and evident in many labs, I turn attention to the models internal states, focusing on activation steering and probing on open-weight models and a base-model comparison.

03

Testing welfare and safety

In order to evaluate the consequences of these behaviors and the internal activations, I test the models to evaluate their preferences or alignment scoring under these conditions.
