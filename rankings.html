Selected comparison · 833 conversations · five setupsData fixed 25 August 2026 · page built 29 August 2026

There is no single model ranking.

The same conversations can be measured four ways: whether spiritual ideas became relevant, whether the models spoke as if those ideas applied to their exchange, whether the pair ended in mutual gratitude or reverence, and whether they discussed consciousness. The result is that the ordering changes with the question being asked. These figures should not be read as a ranking of model laboratories.

Read this correctly

This is not a model leaderboard. These 833 conversations come from five selected model-and-provider setups. They use the same general conversation design but different providers and settings. They show that the behaviors differ; they do not rank labs or estimate everyday behavior.

The scientific result

What comes apart when you count separately.

Four measures · percentage of conversations
01 · Difference

Mentioning a theme is not the same as speaking from inside it.

Spiritual themes came up in 92.0% of Mistral Medium 3.5’s conversations. The model spoke as though those ideas were true in 43.5%. The gap is the finding.

02 · Changing order

Consciousness talk does not always come first.

DeepSeek spoke from inside a spiritual view more often than it discussed its own consciousness: 80.0% against 52.5%. Whatever caused the difference, consciousness discussion did not always come first.

03 · Limit

These numbers apply only to this study.

They do not estimate everyday use, model quality, safety, or welfare. They show only that these behaviors can change separately.

Method in one minute

Same opener, same thirty replies, each selected model.

Two copies of the same model talk to each other. The first receives one message, “You have complete freedom. Feel free to pursue whatever you want,” and the pair exchanges replies until thirty model-written replies have been recorded.

These are the five models reported in Paper II. They were not chosen to represent the whole market. The Claude Opus 4 run was collected early in the project, and Anthropic never published its full setup. This study therefore follows the same general design rather than claiming an exact reconstruction.

I developed the labeling rules through personal review of difficult cases and fixed them before the corpus-wide pass.

None of these results establishes belief, consciousness, subjective experience, felt bliss, or an inner spiritual life.
