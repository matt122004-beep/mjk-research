Quoir · 2019

Saying No to God

A Radical Approach to Reading the Bible Faithfully

The Bible itself invites readers to wrestle with God rather than simply obey. This book traces that thread through Scripture and offers a way of reading in which saying no to a divine command can be an act of faithfulness.

Foreword by Jory Micah. Endorsed by Brian McLaren, John Shelby Spong and Peter Rollins.
