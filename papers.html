Spirituality Seriously

“Spiritual Behavior” in Large Language Models and Its Relevance for AI Welfare and Alignment

Anthropic kept measuring a spiritual-behavior category across changing evaluations, but the published numbers do not all measure the same thing. This paper reconstructs that record and identifies the controlled tests needed next.

Paper IIClassification from observed data

Not Just Claude

Recognizing and Classifying “Spiritual Behavior” in Large Language Models

Across 21,292 conversations in 123 same-model groups, spiritual ideas became relevant far more often than the models spoke from inside them. Adoption was concentrated, and later Claude groups retained consciousness talk even as adoption approached zero.

What the papers do not claim

These papers study the words and actions produced by models. They do not claim that a model believes, experiences, or possesses the spiritual states named in the words it produced.
